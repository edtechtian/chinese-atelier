# Content attribution

Chinese Atelier is an educational application. The 20 lessons of 《走遍泰国》 are attributed to Prince of Songkla University Phuket Confucius Institute. Source-derived text, glosses and retained image derivatives keep their source references. New teaching activities and illustrations are supplementary and remain pending teacher approval.

This repository does not grant a new license over third-party textbook material or embedded images. The original PPTX files and full-resolution slide/image archive are not included. Obtain the original teaching materials separately with appropriate permission. No general open-content license is asserted over the curriculum.

Actual student progress, written submissions, teacher credentials, backups and QA screenshots are excluded. Any student answers appearing in automated tests are fictional QA fixtures.
