# Lesson 3 prototype review

Review date: 2026-10-29. Prototype: **到酒店了**, `zt-03`, “Arrival, clarity & consent”, 65 minutes. This is an editorial and application review, not teacher approval or a trial with Emmalyne.

## Source and selection

Original file: `第3课 到酒店了.pptx`, 13 slides. The original source dataset remains separate in `curriculum/lessons.json`. The prototype was implemented and checked before extending the teaching structure to all 20 lessons.

| Source | Retained content | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slide 5 | Arrival, welcome and request to check in | Identify speaker purpose and distinguish arrival from location |
| Slide 9 | Passport exchange, two rooms, total 3,000 baht, room cards | Explain the unit of the total and identify unknown nights/room type |
| Slide 11 | Permission to enter and luggage delivery | Practise refusal, clarification and a later delivery time |
| Slides 7 and 12 | 一共 examples and 到 + place + 了 | Controlled retrieval, meaning contrasts and connected application |
| Slides 3, 6, 8 and 10 | Source vocabulary and card expressions | Optional pinyin/Thai, contextual retrieval and editorial chunks |

The 100-baht example on slide 7 and 3,000-baht dialogue total on slide 9 are distinct examples. No nightly rate or current hotel price is inferred. Slide 4’s `远动员` is preserved and flagged as an apparent typo for `运动员`; identifying it is not a scored item.

## Complete teaching sequence

1. **Understand, 20 minutes:** diagnostic interpretation of the total, three source dialogues, vocabulary retrieval and evidence-based discussion.
2. **Communicate, 20 minutes:** arrival/location and permission practice, live listening with a partner, changing roles and handling a refusal.
3. **Apply professionally, 25 minutes:** an 80–120-character interpretation, a 2–3-minute role-play with a saved excerpt/reflection, and a 180–260-character guest message plus internal handover.

The unit has ten objective items with server-side answer keys and explanations. Every task has source references, success criteria, optional scaffolds, an extension and a newly authored model. The speaking model is an example exchange, not a complete two-minute performance. Teacher assessment uses the dimensions selected for each task.

## Accuracy and difficulty decisions

- Chinese source readings are retained rather than replaced by invented dialogues. English explanations and new tasks carry separate provenance labels.
- 到…了 reports arrival. 在… reports location. The material does not incorrectly declare 到了酒店 ungrammatical.
- Reading fluency leads to inference, omitted information, audience choices and repair. It does not establish listening or spontaneous-speaking proficiency.
- Pinyin and Thai are optional support. An optional Thai/German comparison examines request strategies without translating every line.
- Correctness feedback addresses meaning and units. New activities extend to guest consent and professional tone without attributing those new exchanges to the source.
- Prices and booking details are training context. Source facts and unresolved facts remain separate.

## Checks and fixes

Browser interaction confirmed that original Chinese renders, an incorrect answer receives an explanation, a corrected retry updates feedback, draft saving survives reload, and a model stays gated until a draft exists. Scaffolds, discussion prompts, success criteria and the live listening plan render in the lesson.

Fixes included clearer original/new labels, contextualized exercises, task-specific assessment dimensions, the correct cookie scope for teacher source downloads, corrected responsive CSS, a discard control for recovered browser drafts, and a recovery indicator that distinguishes a browser copy from saved server work. The prototype interpretation model was rewritten to remove redundant wording during final review.

Responsive checks: 375 px phone, 768 px tablet and 1366 px laptop, with no horizontal page overflow on the inspected pages. Screenshots are in `docs/qa`. These are representative viewport checks, not a claim that every device or every interaction has been tested.

## Remaining review

The teacher should review English glosses, language explanations, new models and assessments before marking the lesson teacher-approved. Local slide previews have font limitations; use the preserved Chinese/Thai text and original PPT for source checking. Listening requires a live partner or teacher. The app neither records nor automatically grades speech, and its writing practice checks are not a teacher or official IB grade.
